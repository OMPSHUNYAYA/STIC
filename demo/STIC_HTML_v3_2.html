STIC — Correctness Without Cloud

Structural Integrity without Cloud. This demo shows the key distinction: dependencies may affect availability, but correctness is resolved from structure.
Core Principle
system_correctness = resolve(structure)
correctness_visible iff structure_complete AND structure_consistent
Structure Inputs

Structure inputs determine STIC correctness. Missing structure becomes ABSTAIN. Conflicting structure becomes BLOCKED.

Dependencies — Toggle ON/OFF

Dependency toggles affect the traditional comparison path. STIC correctness remains invariant because its certificate depends only on structure.
